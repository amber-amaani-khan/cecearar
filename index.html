Open when you miss me

My love,

If you're reading this, I imagine you're missing me a little extra today. And if I could have my way, I wouldn't want you to have to miss me at all. I'd just appear beside you, wrap my arms around you, pull you close, and stay there for as long as you needed.

I think one of the hardest things about loving someone this much is realising that sometimes love can't make the distance disappear. I can't always reach across the room and hold your hand. I can't spontaneously come over, sit beside you, or look at you and know exactly what you're thinking without you saying a word. And I hate that sometimes.

But I hope you know that distance doesn't make me feel any less close to you.

If anything, being away from you will probably make me realise even more just how much of my life has become intertwined with yours. There are going to be so many tiny moments when I instinctively want to turn to you, tell you something ridiculous, show you something I found, or simply sit beside you in comfortable silence. And every one of those moments will remind me how lucky I am that I have someone I miss this much.

Before you, I don't think I really understood what it meant to feel completely connected to another person. With you, it feels like I found someone whose brain and heart somehow speak the same strange language as mine. You make me feel understood in ways I didn't even know I was looking for.

So when you miss me, please don't only think about how far apart we are.

Think about how incredibly lucky we are that, out of all the people in this enormous world, we somehow found each other.

Think about our stupid jokes, our endless conversations, our gaming nights, our little adventures, our picnics, our food dates, and all the ordinary moments that somehow became my favourite memories simply because they were with you.

Close your eyes and imagine me sitting beside you. Imagine my hand in yours. Imagine me looking at you with that ridiculous amount of love I have for you.

I'm still there.

And no amount of miles could ever make you any less mine to love.

I love you. I miss you. And I'll be counting down until I can hold you again.
